GATE CS → PSU Jobs: Complete Research Guide

Stream: Computer Science & Engineering (GATE paper code: CS) Compiled: 10 September 2026 Scope: Every major PSU + Group-A organisation that recruits CSE/IT graduates via GATE CS

How to read this: Cutoffs and salaries are drawn from published notifications, RTI-based reports, and coaching/edtech compilations for GATE 2023–2026. Where a figure is an estimate or projection, it is marked [est]. Always verify the live notification on the PSU's own careers page before applying — PSU hiring rules change every cycle.

PART 0 — The Single Most Important Reality Check

Before the list, understand these five facts. They decide your whole strategy.

  1. very few PSUs hire CSE. Most PSUs exist for oil, power, steel, mining, defence hardware. Their core cadres are Mechanical, Electrical, Civil, Chemical, Instrumentation. CSE seats are typically 1–10 per cycle, sometimes zero. This is why CS cutoffs are sky-high.

  2. High marks ≠ high competition-free. Because seats are few, a CS cutoff can be higher than Mechanical/Electrical (e.g. GAIL CS 2024 = 88.6 marks vs GAIL Mechanical 89.3 — near-parity despite far fewer seats).

  3. Several top PSUs have moved OFF GATE to their own CBT. IOCL (2025 & 2026), ONGC (2025), HPCL, BEL, ECIL largely run their own Computer-Based Test now. GATE still works for many, but always check whether the current cycle is "through GATE" or "through CBT".

  4. GATE score is only the entry ticket. Typical weightage: GATE 85% + GD 3–5% + Interview 10–12%. So your GATE marks do ~85% of the work — but interview prep decides the final seat.

  5. Score validity: a GATE scorecard is officially valid 3 years, but most PSUs accept only the current year's score. Assume your score lives for one hiring cycle.

PART 2 — Detailed Cards (the ones worth chasing)

🥇 Tier 1 — Best pay + brand for CSE

1. GAIL (India) Limited — the best "pure CSE" PSU seat

  • Role: Executive Trainee (BIS — Business Information System), E-2 grade
  • Why CSE-heavy: GAIL is one of the very few PSUs where a Computer Science degree is the required qualification for a specific post (not just "eligible").
  • Eligibility: B.E./B.Tech in Computer Science, min 65% marks; age ~26–28 (relaxations for OBC +3, SC/ST +5, PwD +10)
  • Cutoff: 88.6 marks (GATE 2024) — among the highest of any PSU discipline. [est] 85–90 for 2025–26.
  • Pay: Basic ₹60,000 in scale ₹60,000–1,80,000 during 1-year training-cum-probation; confirmed into same scale (E-2). Annual CTC ≈ ₹15–22 LPA with DA/HRA/perks.
  • Bond: 3 years typical.
  • Selection: GATE marks (high weight) → GD → Interview.
  • Career: IT/ERP/SCADA/cyber roles across pipelines & corporate offices. Postings: Noida HQ + regional.
  • Verdict: If you want CSE-as-CSE in a Maharatna, GAIL BIS is the flagship target. Also expect the toughest CS cutoff.

2. ONGC — highest CTC

  • Role: Assistant Executive Engineer (Computer Science / IT), E-1 level
  • Eligibility: B.E./B.Tech Computer Engg / IT, min 60%; MCA / PG Computer Science / DOEACC 'B'-level also eligible (rare and useful if you have MCA)
  • Cutoff: 63 marks (General, 2022 cycle) for CSE
  • Pay: Basic ₹60,000–1,80,000 + 3% annual increment; allowances @35% of basic under cafeteria + DA + HRA/company accommodation + CPF + PRP + gratuity + medical. ~₹25 LPA CTC — the best headline package among CS-eligible PSUs.
  • Age: up to 30 (generous).
  • Bond: 3 years.
  • Note: ONGC ran its own CBT in 2025 for E1 posts; check whether your cycle is GATE-based or CBT-based.

3. IOCL — biggest recruiter by volume

  • Role: Engineer/Officer Grade-A (+ Graduate Apprentice Engineer Scheme, GAES)
  • Eligibility: B.E./B.Tech CS with 65% (55% SC/ST/PwD); age ≤26
  • Cutoff: RTI reports suggest a lower-than-expected shortlist cutoff (~54 marks) because so many posts were filled.
  • Pay: Scale ₹60,000–1,80,000; annual CTC ₹16–25 LPA. (GAES trainees get a lump-sum monthly stipend for 1 year first.)
  • Bond: 3 years; bond amount ₹3,00,000 (Gen/EWS/OBC) / ₹50,000 (SC/ST/PwBD) for Grade-A.
  • Selection: Historically GATE weight 85% + GD/GT 5% + PI 10%. IOCL 2025 & 2026 moved to its own CBT (85% CBT + 5% GD + 10% PI) — so check the notification year carefully.
  • Verdict: High volume, strong package, but verify GATE-vs-CBT each cycle.

🥈 Tier 2 — Strong, stable, solid pay

4. NTPC — largest vacancy count

  • Role: Engineering Executive Trainee (EET), E-1
  • Disciplines: Electrical, Mechanical, Civil, Electronics/Instrumentation, Computer Science/IT
  • Vacancies: ~475–515 per cycle (biggest of all, though CS share is small)
  • Pay: ₹40,000–1,40,000; CTC ₹12–18 LPA
  • Bond: 3 years; ₹5,00,000 (Gen/EWS/OBC), ₹2,50,000 (SC/ST/PwBD)
  • Age: 27
  • Selection: GATE score (no separate exam) + GD + PI; ~85% GATE weight.

5. POWERGRID (PGCIL) — transparent cutoffs

  • Role: Engineer Trainee (Computer Science)
  • Cutoff (GATE 2024, CS): Gen 67.88, OBC 57.4, SC 50.73, ST 40.15, EWS 57.12. 2025 Gen ≈ 70 — trending up.
  • Pay: ₹40,000–1,40,000 (IDA) during 1-year training; post-training CTC ≈ ₹22.5 LPA.
  • Bond: 3 years; ₹5,00,000 (Gen/OBC/EWS), ₹2,50,000 (SC/ST/PwBD).
  • Age: 28.
  • Catch: CS vacancies are tiny (e.g. only 2 posts in 2025, routed via CTUIL) — so cutoff is volatile.

6. Grid India (POSOCO) — prestige + strong pay

  • Role: Executive Trainee
  • Disciplines: primarily Electrical; CS/Computer Engg accepted in some cycles
  • Cutoff (2024): Gen 74.67, OBC 72.67, SC 60.33, ST 58.67
  • Pay: E-2 ₹50,000–1,60,000 during 1-year on-the-job training
  • Age: 28; min 65% in degree.
  • Verdict: Elite grid-operation role; CS intake intermittent — watch the notification.

7. BARC (OCES/DGFS) — best "research" CS route

  • Role: Scientific Officer 'C' (Group-A, DAE) — CS discipline code 26
  • Route: GATE score or BARC's own online exam → interview
  • CS GATE cutoff 2025: 831 (CBT cutoff 124/300)
  • Stipend: ₹74,000/month + ₹30,000 book allowance during 1-year training (OCES)
  • After absorption: Level-10 ₹56,100 + increments → ~₹1,34,000/month gross in Mumbai; Group-A Gazetted, air-travel allowance, health scheme.
  • Bond: indemnity — serve DAE 3 years after training.
  • Verdict: A research-grade CS career (HPC, cyber, control systems, computational science). CS cutoff is high but the payoff is excellent.

8. DRDO (via RAC) — defence research

  • Role: Scientist 'B' (also ADA, WESEE posts)
  • Vacancies: ~148–181 per cycle across disciplines incl Computer Science
  • Eligibility: first-class B.E./B.Tech CS (65%)
  • Pay: Level-10 ₹56,100–1,77,000; gross ~₹1,00,000/month
  • Age: 28
  • GATE: used for shortlisting; score >700–800 considered competitive
  • Verdict: Great for cyber/embedded/AI research interest; own exam/ interview heavy.

PART 4 — Selection Process (common pattern)

  1. GATE — appear in the correct paper (CS) and qualify.
  2. Apply on the PSU's careers portal with your GATE registration number.
  3. Shortlist on GATE score/marks (category-wise).
  4. Group Discussion / Group Task (some PSUs skip this).
  5. Personal Interview — technical + HR.
  6. Medical fitness check.
  7. Offer & joining (often 1-year training-cum-probation first).

Weightage examples: PGCIL = GATE 85% + GD 3% + PI 12%. IOCL old = GATE 85% + GD 5% + PI 10%. → GATE does ~85% of the work; the interview decides the final seat.

PART 5 — Salary Reality: CTC vs In-Hand vs Pay Scale

PSU CTC figures are inflated by DA + HRA + PRP + PF + gratuity + medical. What actually matters:

  • Pay scale (e.g. ₹60,000–1,80,000) = the basic-pay band you sit in, with 3% annual increments.
  • In-hand ≈ basic + DA + HRA − deductions. For a ₹60,000-basic PSU job, in-hand is roughly ₹85,000–₹1,10,000/month depending on city (metro HRA higher).
  • CTC (₹15–25 LPA) includes employer PF, gratuity, medical, PRP — treat as ~60–70% real cash.
  • Rough CTC by pay scale (2025): ₹40,000 scale ≈ ₹15 LPA · ₹50,000 scale ≈ ₹19 LPA · ₹60,000 scale ≈ ₹26 LPA.

Non-monetary value: job security, subsidised housing/medical, pension/gratuity, fixed postings, low work pressure, PRP bonus. This is why PSUs compete with private IT even at lower cash.

PART 6 — Strategy Notes for a CSE Aspirant

  1. Target realistically. GAIL BIS is the cleanest CSE fit; ONGC AEE(CS) has the best CTC; BARC/DRDO suit research-minded CS. NTPC/PGCIL/NPCIL are high-volume-but-few-CS-seats backups.
  2. Aim 70+ marks / AIR <600 to be comfortably shortlisted for a top PSU in CS.
  3. Track notifications March–June each year (GATE results ~March; PSU notices March–May; interviews May–July; joining June–August).
  4. Watch the GATE-vs-CBT switch. IOCL, ONGC, HPCL, BEL, ECIL have run their own CBTs — if a PSU has gone CBT, your GATE score becomes irrelevant for that cycle.
  5. Keep M.Tech/PG as a parallel option. A high GATE CS score simultaneously opens IIT/IISc M.Tech and CSIR/PSU research — CS PSU seats alone are too few to rely on 100%.
  6. Don't ignore MCA/PG eligibility. ONGC explicitly accepts MCA and PG-Computer Science / DOEACC 'B' level — a second eligibility lever.
  7. Interview prep = 15% of score but decides the seat. Prepare: your B.Tech project, OS/DBMS/Networks/DS/Algorithms fundamentals, current affairs of the sector.
  8. Verify, don't trust blogs. Cutoffs on edtech sites are often estimates. Cross-check the PSU's official PDF notification.
