📘 Your Subjects

GATE CS = 65 questions / 100 marks: General Aptitude 15 + Engineering Mathematics 13 + Core CS 72. Tackle them in the order below — it front-loads the highest-yield and most foundational subjects first.

S01
Engineering Mathematics
13 marks

Discrete Maths, Linear Algebra, Calculus, Probability

S02
Digital Logic
7 marks

Boolean & K-map, combinational/sequential design, number systems, CMOS & timing

S03
Computer Organization & Architecture
8 marks

Instructions & addressing, ALU/data-path/control, pipelining, memory hierarchy, I/O (interrupt & DMA)

S04
Programming & Data Structures
9 marks

Programming in C, recursion, arrays, stacks, queues, linked lists, trees, BSTs, heaps, graphs

S05
Algorithms
10 marks

Complexity, Recurrences, Sorting, Hashing, Greedy/DP/D&C, Graphs, MST, Shortest Paths

S06
Theory of Computation
8 marks

Finite automata, regex, CFG/PDA, pumping lemma, Turing machines, undecidability

S07
Compiler Design
6 marks

Lexical analysis, parsing (LL/LR), SDT, runtime environments, IR, optimisation & data-flow

S08
Operating Systems
8 marks

processes & threads, CPU scheduling, synchronisation, deadlock, memory & virtual memory, file systems

S09
Databases
8 marks

ER model, relational algebra & SQL, normal forms, B/B+ indexing, transactions & concurrency

S10
Computer Networks
8 marks

Layering, switching & performance, data link, routing, IP addressing/CIDR/NAT, transport (TCP/UDP/flow/congestion), application (DNS/SMTP/HTTP/FTP)
